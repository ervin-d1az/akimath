# A week of each format

## Why

Every format has a generator now, and the pack still carries what was typed by hand: four
KenKens, four Killers, and **one each** of the magic square, Kakuro and the sopa de letras. The
home offers one board per format per day, so three of the five formats repeat tomorrow.

## What changes

- **Seven boards of every format**, so a week passes before any of them comes round again.
- Sizes vary within each format, so a week is not the same board at a different seed.
- A gate names the constraint that caught a bad batch during this change: **no board may ask for
  a digit the keypad cannot enter.**

## Out of scope

More than seven. The rotation gate asks that every board be offered within a fortnight, and
fifteen of a kind would quietly break it — which is the right thing for it to do, and the reason
to grow the pack deliberately rather than by as much as the generators can produce.
